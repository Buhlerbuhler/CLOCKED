How to use it

Drag the dial in the direction shown. Hold each combination number still for one second to continue.

  1. Reset: turn right three full times. The trainer will return the dial to 0.
  2. Turn right to the first number.
  3. Turn left, pass the first number once, then stop at the second.
  4. Turn right directly to the third number and pull the lock open.

You can also use the arrow keys. Press D to watch a demonstration.
