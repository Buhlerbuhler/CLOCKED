GOAL: GET THE VARIABLE BY ITSELF.

Both sides begin equal, so the scale begins balanced. Change one side and the scale will tilt.

Make the same mathematical change to the other side to restore the balance.

Enter operations using +5, -5, /5, or *5.

SHOW VALUE reveals the hidden value represented by the variable. A coefficient such as 5m will display as five separate boxes so students can still see that there are five equal groups.

SHOW WORKING opens conventional algebra working beside the balance. Cancellation marks appear on the exact part being undone.
